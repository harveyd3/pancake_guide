# How to Make Pancakes 

## Introduction
In this guide, you will be learning how to make a pancake step-by-step. This tutorial will help you make the best pancakes known to man!

## Ingredients Needed
- [ ] Pancake Mix (Make sure it is the mix that only needs water)
- [ ] PAM Spray
- [ ] Water

## Equipment Needed
- [ ] Stove
- [ ] Pan
- [ ] Bowl
- [ ] Spatula
- [ ] Whisk
- [ ] Measuring Cup

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Spray the pan with PAM spray to prevent the pancakes from sticking. Reapply for each pancake as needed.
2. Measure the water and pancake mix according to the package instructions, depending on how many pancakes you want to make.
3. Mix the pancake mix and water together using a whisk. The batter should have a texture similar to cake batter.
4. Pour the batter onto the pan, stopping when the pancake reaches your desired size.
5. Flip the pancake when bubbles form on top and the edges begin to look set.
6. After flipping, cook the other side until the pancake is golden brown and the center is fully cooked. Then serve it.

## Troubleshooting Common Pancake Problems
1. Batter is too runny: Add a little more pancake mix at a time and stir until you get the consistency you want.
